## ⚖️ About Us

**BAWASLU-M FH ULM** (Badan Pengawas Pemilihan Umum Mahasiswa Fakultas Hukum Universitas Lambung Mangkurat) is the official supervisory institution responsible for overseeing the student elections (PEMILUMA) at the Faculty of Law[cite: 1, 4, 11, 12]. 

Initiated by the **2026 BAWASLU-M FH ULM Leadership**[cite: 7], this digital profile is a strategic step toward digitalization and the optimal utilization of modern media. This GitHub account was specifically established and dedicated as a tangible manifestation of our transparency. We aim to position BAWASLU-M FH ULM 2026 as an innovative, modern, and highly transparent election supervisory body that guarantees a fair, clean, and highly legitimate election process.

---

## 🏛️ Organizational Structure 2026

Our 2026 organization is built upon a strong foundation divided into executive leadership and specialized functional divisions to ensure maximum election integrity[cite: 7].

| Position | Role / Division |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Executive Board** | Led by the Chairperson (Ketua Umum), supported by the Secretary (Sekretaris Umum) and Treasurer (Bendahara Umum)[cite: 1, 7]. |
| **Divisi Maintara** | Information Management, Administration, and Relational Governance. Focuses on public relations, document archiving, and social media (Sub-divisions: Makro & Podkes)[cite: 1, 7]. |
| **Divisi Hukum** | Legal Drafting, Prevention, and Violation Handling. Responsible for election regulations and dispute resolution (Sub-divisions: Penanganan & Advokasi)[cite: 1, 7]. |

## 🌐 2026 Digitalization & Transparency Commitment

In 2026, BAWASLU-M FH ULM is taking a major leap toward open governance. We believe that a democratic election requires absolute public trust, which can only be achieved through transparency and continuous innovation. 

*   **Open Data & Regulations:** Utilizing GitHub as a centralized repository to publish election rules, official decisions, and supervisory guidelines, ensuring they are transparent and accessible to every FH ULM student.
*   **Digital Monitoring:** Maximizing the use of digital platforms to monitor campaign activities and receive reports on alleged election violations swiftly.
*   **Interactive Public Relations:** Shifting from traditional announcements to engaging digital content via Instagram, TikTok, and Canva-designed infographics to educate the student body about their voting rights and election rules.
