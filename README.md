## ⚖️ About Us

**BAWASLU-M FH ULM** (Badan Pengawas Pemilihan Umum Mahasiswa Fakultas Hukum Universitas Lambung Mangkurat) is the official supervisory institution responsible for overseeing the student elections (PEMILUMA) at the Faculty of Law. 

Initiated by the **2026 BAWASLU-M FH ULM Leadership**, this digital profile is a strategic step toward digitalization and the optimal utilization of modern media. We aim to position BAWASLU-M FH ULM 2026 as an innovative, modern, and highly transparent election supervisory body that guarantees a fair, clean, and highly legitimate election process.

### 👁️ Vision
To become a professional and high-integrity student election supervisory body.
### 🎯 Mission
*   Ensuring the professional supervision of the Faculty of Law student elections.
*   Coordinating the internal work of BAWASLU-M FH ULM efficiently.
*   Representing the institution in external affairs and maintaining strong cooperative relationships with external parties.

## 🏛️ Organizational Structure & Duties

Our 2026 organization is built upon a strong foundation divided into executive leadership and specialized functional divisions.

### Executive Board (Badan Pengurus Harian)
*   **Ketua Umum:** Coordinates the work of the institution, represents BAWASLU-M FH ULM in external affairs, and holds ultimate responsibility for all organizational activities.
*   **Sekretaris Umum:** Responsible for all secretarial administration, managing incoming/outgoing correspondence, and substituting for the Chairperson when necessary.
*   **Bendahara Umum:** Manages organizational finances with the Chairperson's approval, coordinates the treasury, and establishes financial management mechanisms.

### Functional Divisions
*   **Divisi Maintara (Information Management & Administration):** Documents and publishes work activities, archives data, provides public information regarding activities, and publishes commemorative holiday greetings. Contains sub-divisions *Makro* and *Podkes*.
*   **Divisi Hukum (Legal Division):** Responsible for legal advocacy, drafting the Student Election Supervisory Regulations (PBWU), and preventing alleged election violations. Contains sub-divisions *Penanganan* and *Advokasi*.
