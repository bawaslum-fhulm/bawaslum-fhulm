## ⚖️ About Us

**BAWASLU-M FH ULM** (Badan Pengawas Pemilihan Umum Mahasiswa Fakultas Hukum Universitas Lambung Mangkurat) is the official supervisory institution responsible for overseeing the student elections (PEMILUMA) at the Faculty of Law, Lambung Mangkurat University. We operate on the principles of independence, legal certainty, orderliness, proportionality, and professionalism.

- 👁️ **Vision:** To become a professional and high-integrity student election supervisory body.
- 🎯 **Mission:** Ensuring fair election supervision, coordinating institutional work, and building strong, transparent relationships with external parties.
- 📍 **Headquarters:** Jl. Brigjen H. Hasan Basri Kayu Tangi, Banjarmasin, Kode Pos 70123.

---

## 🏛️ Organizational Structure

Our organization is built upon a strong foundation divided into executive leadership and specialized functional divisions to ensure maximum election integrity.

| Position | Role / Division |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Executive Board** | Comprises the Chairperson (Ketua Umum), Secretary (Sekretaris Umum), and Treasurer (Bendahara Umum). |
| **Divisi Maintara** | Information Management, Administration, and Relational Governance. Focuses on public relations, document archiving, and social media (Sub-divisions: Makro & Podkes). |
| **Divisi Hukum** | Legal Drafting, Prevention, and Violation Handling. Responsible for election regulations and dispute resolution (Sub-divisions: Penanganan & Advokasi). |

## 📋 Featured Programs & Agendas

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 PEMILUMA Supervision & Monitoring</b></summary>
<br/>

> Comprehensive monitoring of the student election timeline, from candidate registration to the final voting process.

- **Digital Campaign Monitoring:** Tracking official candidate social media accounts and ensuring compliance with the #DemokrasiFakultasMerah hashtag rules.
- **Violation Handling:** Providing a clear flow for reports, clarifications, and dispute resolutions regarding election violations.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📢 Open Recruitment & Volunteer Management</b></summary>
<br/>

> Engaging active students of FH ULM to participate as official volunteers and Panwaslu-M members to expand our supervisory reach.

- **Scope:** Selecting and training dedicated students to uphold democracy within the faculty.
- **Output:** Issuing official Member Identity Cards (KTA) and comprehensive supervision training.
</details>
